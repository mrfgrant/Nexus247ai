The first is 38 CFR § 3.303(b) — continuity of symptomatology. This is the path for veterans who don't have a clear in-service event on record. It requires showing continuous symptoms from service to present. Most AI tools ignore it entirely because it's harder to template. If your letter generation doesn't explicitly address this for veterans with documentation gaps, you're leaving a significant population underserved.
The second is 38 CFR § 3.102 — benefit of the doubt. This is the legal standard that should anchor every letter. "At least as likely as not" is the threshold, not "definitely caused by." If your letters aren't explicitly invoking this standard in the nexus language, raters can dismiss them on a technicality even when the medical argument is sound.
The third is DBQ alignment. VA raters work from Disability Benefits Questionnaires. A nexus letter that doesn't map its arguments to the specific DBQ criteria for that condition gets set aside. Your C&P prep tool touches this, but the letter generation itself should be cross-referencing DBQ criteria as a quality check — that's what "rater readiness" in your RPA score should be measuring.
The fourth is 38 CFR § 3.400 — effective dates. Veterans chronically lose money not because they lose claims but because they file too late or don't understand what triggers an earlier effective date. An AOD motion, a CUE claim, a continuous pursuit argument — these are money on the table that most veterans never know to pursue. You have AOD in Concierge, which is good. But the app should probably surface effective date analysis automatically, not just as a premium feature.
The biggest unmet need in the veteran claims space:
It's not letter generation. Every tool does that now. The unmet need is post-award claim development — helping veterans who already have a rating understand what they're still leaving on the table. SMC, TDIU, secondary conditions from already-rated primaries, 100% schedular vs. TDIU, Aid & Attendance eligibility. Most veterans at 70% have no idea they may qualify for TDIU at 100% pay. Most veterans with mobility issues have no idea SMC-L exists.
Your Concierge tier touches this. But it should be a core feature visible to every user the moment they log in — a "what you might be missing" analysis based on their current rating and conditions.
